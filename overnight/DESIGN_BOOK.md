# SEER DESIGN BOOK (binding for the landing AND every product UI)
The landing page /seer-2026 is the design book. Open the screenshots shots/land_d*.png and read src/seer-2026 (tokens, type, motion, glass) before designing anything. Product UIs must feel like the same company: same typeface (Inter Tight), same violet (#6C47FF family) used as a controlled accent, white-first light theme, charcoal (#0E0E12) dark theme, same soft depth, same easing (power3/power4 out, 0.4–0.9 s), same restraint.
## The owner's brief (X post, binding)
"Keep both sides of Seer's marketplace in mind: users who need information to make decisions, and traders who put capital behind the forecasts that help provide it. Interfaces must be optimised for both. The seer app (app-seer-pm) should be Seer's equivalent of Etherscan: a general explorer for all Seer markets, where people can find and trade across market types rather than being limited to one. Get creative; fresh ideas, not Polymarket reskins. Use Electric Foundation's brand kit as the starting point; explain deliberate departures." (EF kit: ../EF_KIT_VALUES.md, ../assets/ef_kit, ../docs/ef-kit-real.md.)
## Product rules
1. MINIMAL, NOT CLUTTERED. One screen = one job. Max ~2 viewport-heights of vertical scroll on any primary page; use tabs, drawers, popovers, command palette, split panes, progressive disclosure instead of long pages. One primary action per view. Generous whitespace, 8px grid, consistent 16/24/32/48 rhythm, clear hierarchy (one large number or headline per area).
2. EASY TO UNDERSTAND. Plain language, no Web3 jargon where avoidable (show "Chance 62%" not "implied probability"), explain with tooltips. Every control labelled; every state visible (loading, empty, error).
3. LIGHT AND DARK both first-class, toggle persisted, designed separately (dark = charcoal surfaces with hairline borders at 8% alpha and violet accent, NOT blue-black or neon). Check contrast >= 4.5:1 in both.
4. NO FEATURE LOSS. Every feature in the existing product (../apps/<name>/src, ../products/<name>/REQUIREMENTS.md, DIRECTION.md, live-product notes in ../docs/live-products.md and ../products/<name>/audit*) must exist in the redesign, even small ones (e.g. CSV download inside a market, filters, links to Kleros/Reality.eth/explorers, collections, share). Layout changes, features do not. Keep a FEATURES.md checklist per product and tick items only when implemented and reachable in the UI.
5. REAL CONTENT AND IMAGERY. Use realistic market data (questions, odds, volume, liquidity, resolution dates) and market photos/avatars. Look for existing assets in ../apps/*/public, ../apps/*/src, ../products/*/data, ../research, ../kit; if a market has no photo, generate a tasteful tonal placeholder (soft duotone gradient with subject initial), never stock clip-art or emoji icons.
6. NO SLOP. No generic card grids of identical boxes, no neon gradients, no glow blobs, no decorative icon clutter, no fake "AI" widgets, no numbered labels, no lorem. Cards (where used) are information-dense, compact and different in scale by importance. Charts are clean, thin-lined, annotated, interactive (hover crosshair, range switcher).
7. Wallet never faked: "Connect" opens a real flow only if the app supports it; mock signing is rejected honestly.
8. Motion: purposeful and subtle: shared-element transitions, hover drawers, number tickers, smooth theme crossfade, skeletons. Respect prefers-reduced-motion. 60fps (transform/opacity).
9. Responsive: 1440 desktop primary; 390 mobile must be a real mobile layout (bottom sheet trade ticket, sticky bottom action), no horizontal scroll.
10. Quality gates: no overlaps/clipping, no dead controls (every button/link works), keyboard + focus-visible, semantic HTML, `npm run build` passes.
## Implementation contracts
- Theme: `localStorage['seer-theme']` = 'light' | 'dark', applied as `data-theme` on <html> before first paint. Every product reads/writes that key (the screenshot QA sets it).
- Each product lives in src/products/<name>/ with its own FEATURES.md and exports `Page` from index.tsx; shared shell/tokens in src/products/shared/.
## Departures from the EF kit
Allowed only with a one-paragraph reason in src/products/<name>/DEPARTURES.md.

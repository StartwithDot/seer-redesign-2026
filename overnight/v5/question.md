DESIGN: "One Question" (src/seer-2026/designs/question/QuestionLanding.tsx, export QuestionLanding). Type-led, cinematic, NO photographs and no 3D. The page is a film about a single question changing hands.
Hero: near-black page (#0A0A0A) filling the viewport. A giant Instrument Serif question types itself in white, line by line ("Will the DAO fund the Q4 grants round?"), cursor blinking in violet. When it finishes, a big price rises beneath it as one number (62%) in Inter Tight tabular, set very large; a single thin violet line (the EF line motif) enters from the far left edge and is drawn to the number. Small support copy and two actions sit bottom-left; nav top. Prices wander slowly (62 to 65 to 61). Nothing else decorative.
Story (pinned, scrubbed): the question stays pinned centre while the asker changes with scroll: A DAO asks (Futarchy: price if pass vs if fail, two numbers), A treasury asks (Risk: three protocols side by side as three large numbers), An AI agent asks (Deep: a ranked list of eight outcomes set as huge stacked words with percentages), A sponsor asks (Opportunity: three submissions, odds, "Reviewers decide" resolved at the end), Anyone asks (Seer App: a quick ticker-like row of market questions with prices, then "Open Seer App"). Each state typesets its own words in giant type; transitions are word-level masks (line/word slides), not generic fades. Each state ends with one text link to the real app.
Then: statement section on off-white with scroll-scrubbed words ("Markets turn what people know into prices anyone can read."), How it works (Ask, Price, Read) as three giant serif words that fill with ink as you scroll, Kleros as the single charcoal section with a calm typographic verdict ("Disputed? Jurors decide.") and a precise set of nine small squares aligning into a line (jury), then the final CTA and footer with the oversized wordmark. Background tweens black -> off-white -> charcoal -> off-white like a film cut, never boxed.
Keep it typographically superb: huge serif, tight leading (0.92-1.0), generous negative space.

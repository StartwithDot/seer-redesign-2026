# SEER LANDING — ART DIRECTION (binding)
Product: Seer, Information Finance infrastructure. Audience: crypto-native traders AND decision-makers who need information. Bar: multi-billion-dollar product site (think Linear, Stripe, Vercel, Arc, Apple product pages) — NOT an AI template.
Brand: Electric Foundation kit (see ../EF_KIT_VALUES.md, ../assets/ef_kit, tokens in src/design-system/tokens.css): white-first, violet accent (#6C47FF family), Inter Tight display type. Neutral warm whites, charcoal (#0E0E12) only for one dark section. Violet is an accent, never a wash.
## HARD BANS (these are what "AI slop" means here — the owner rejected the previous attempt for them)
- No numbered sections / "01 02 03" labels, no tiny decorative caps labels, no fake stats/logos/testimonials, no emoji.
- No generic card grids, no identical rounded boxes with icon+title+text, no gradient blobs, no glowing orbs, no particle fields, no "dashboard" mock panels, no stock icon sets.
- No wall of grey low-contrast text. Body text must be clearly readable (>=4.5:1). No letter-by-letter gimmicks.
- No flat default-looking WebGL primitives (the owner rejected "tyre/prop" 3D). Hero object is a pre-rendered Blender lens video.
## What GOOD looks like (build these)
- Smooth scrolling (Lenis, lerp ~0.09) wired to GSAP ScrollTrigger. Respect prefers-reduced-motion (disable lenis + scrub, show final states).
- Typography as the hero of every section: very large Inter Tight (clamp 56–168px), tight tracking (-0.04em), weight 600-700, deliberate line breaks, optical sizing. Line-mask reveals (each line slides up from overflow:hidden, 0.9s power4.out, stagger 0.08). Scroll-scrubbed word highlight (words go from 18% to 100% ink as you scroll) for the main statement. One sentence-scale "text swap" flip where the headline word rotates in 3D (rotateX flip, perspective) between "forecasts / signals / markets" style words — only where meaningful.
- Flip effects: 3D flip (rotateY with backface hidden) for app showcase visual swaps; GSAP Flip for layout morphs between the three stages.
- Sections bleed into each other: background colour tweened on scroll (off-white #FAFAFB → soft grey #F0F0F3 → white → charcoal #0E0E12 → white), no hard section borders, overlapping parallax layers, sticky/pinned storytelling with scrubbed timelines.
- Micro-interactions: magnetic primary buttons (translate toward cursor, spring back), arrow slides on hover, underline-draw links, cursor-aware tilt (max 6deg) on the hero lens video container with damping, nav that condenses to a floating glass pill on scroll and hides on scroll-down/shows on scroll-up.
- Glass: real-looking glass shapes made with CSS (backdrop-filter blur+saturate, 1px inner highlights, layered soft shadows, specular gradient) and inline SVG geometry (not clip-art). Soft multi-layer shadows (not single drop-shadow).
- Motion grammar: ease power3/power4 out, durations 0.6–1.1s, scrubbed motion tied to scroll; nothing bounces; nothing loops noisily. 60fps; transform/opacity only.

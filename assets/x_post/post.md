# Seer on X, 17 Oct 2026: "Redesigning Seer for Information Finance (Part II of the Rebrand Contest)"
Source: https://x.com/seer_pm/status/2111351009801089244 (images in this folder: a.png meme "Info Finance", b.png "Prediction markets work at scale" chart, c.png white raven logo + SEER wordmark + preferred line direction)
- Seer moves from the "prediction markets" label to "Information Finance". Prediction markets today are a place to trade, not a way of information discovery (Vitalik: start from something people need to know, design a market that draws out information about it). Stat: <2% of markets produce information (The Block 2026); 77% of Sept volume is sports and parlays; $331B traded Jan to Sep 2026.
- Electric Foundation (EF) was the favourite theme from round one and its identity is the recognisable STARTING POINT. Flexible, creativity encouraged.
- Reverse the direction of EF's line design. The correct direction (image c.png): ONE line enters from the LEFT and FANS OUT into many parallel lines to the RIGHT (one question becomes many outcomes). Violet lines on near-black.
- Design for BOTH the person ASKING the question and the trader. Not Polymarket reskins. A visitor must tell: who is asking the question, what the market is actually pricing, how to use that signal. Someone must understand why a market exists and what they can learn without becoming a trader.
- Direction: the agentic economy (agents ask, forecast, trade). Consider useful agent features.
- Homepage brief (verbatim intent): "introduces Seer as the protocol beneath these applications and shows how they fit together in one ecosystem."
- Interfaces: Seer app = general explorer (Seer's Etherscan); Deep = many-outcome questions incl. markets for AI agents (e.g. agents forecast how human evaluators would assess a large field of grant proposals); Futarchy = conditional markets, what a success measure looks like if proposal passes vs not, informs a DAO without deciding for it; Opportunity Markets = traders forecast which submissions get selected, reviewers keep final say; Risk Pricing = market estimates of risk across protocols, compare instead of one yes/no price.
- Tagline in profile: "Information Finance infrastructure for the agentic economy."

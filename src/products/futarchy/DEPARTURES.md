The Futarchy surface keeps the Electric Foundation's Inter Tight typography, white-first paper, charcoal dark mode, soft depth, restrained violet accent, pill controls, and reversed one-to-many line motif. I depart from the landing composition by making the line motif a semantic Decision Spine rather than decorative geometry, replacing the landing's cinematic hero with a dense governance question and conditional comparison, and using compact evidence-first route surfaces so traders and decision-makers can inspect the same market without a dashboard of generic cards. Market media uses verified Seer artwork from the project asset library, while organization marks remain tonal subject initials because no verified organization portraits are available; the official Seer mark remains the shared brand anchor. Light violet surfaces use #F0EDFF so the #6C47FF labels exceed 4.5:1 contrast; filled controls retain #6C47FF with white text in both themes.

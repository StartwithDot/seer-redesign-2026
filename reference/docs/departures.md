# EF departures

This file records the declared departures from the EF kit, what stays unchanged, and the honesty notes.

| Surface | Departure | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Homepage | Particle-built bird sprite in the hero only as the brand's 'seer' motif; reduced-motion respected | Memorable identity beyond the flat raven; hero only |
| Homepage | Near-black panels with violet lines; no blue panels remain | Keeps every section on the kit surface; violet lines carry the fan-out motif |
| Homepage | Display headings are Inter Tight 500 with tight tracking, no serif | One display face across the site, matching the apps |
| Explorer | Left 'Top markets by volume' rail and flat probability bars instead of dense tables; Yes/No tones are violet and grey tonal steps | Shows the answer first (percentages), cards over rows |
| Risk | Sequential violet scale from light violet low risk to deep violet high risk instead of traffic-light colours | Stays inside the kit's single hue |
| Deep | Violet chart; default shows top 10 repositories plus a median band; 'All' reveals all 98 lines | A readable chart: ten leaders plus a median band, instead of 98 overlapping lines |
| Narrow screens | Product nav becomes a horizontally scrollable single row and tables become labelled cards or stacked rows | Keeps the wordmark and nav visible at 390px without a hamburger |
| Wordmark and raven | Our rendition; hex values and fonts are our reading of the public kit boards, not exported tokens | The public kit exposes no production logo or exported tokens |

## What follows the EF kit unchanged

- Shared shell in all five apps: flat raven + SEER wordmark linking home, one product nav (Explorer, Deep, Futarchy, Opportunity, Risk), one Connect button, one footer line 'Seer. Information Finance.' with the five links.
- Tokens and fonts: Inter Tight display and text, JetBrains Mono for numerals.
- Violet #7A3CFF primary.
- Near-black surfaces.

## Honesty notes

- Real data snapshots: Deep uses the real deep.seer.pm API snapshot (98 repositories); Explorer uses real app.seer.pm market snapshots; Opportunity uses real submissions; Risk uses the real Credora/Seer snapshot for 33 assets, Q3 2026 closed with final prices.
- Opportunity uses sample price history and simulated trades, labelled.
- No live wallet or live trading anywhere; Connect opens simulated flows or links to Seer.
- Futarchy never overrules the DAO; a DAO asks what happens to the token if a proposal passes, and the DAO still votes.

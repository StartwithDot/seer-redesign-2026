Trade with Ease

Buy and sell shares with ease, monitor real-time data, and make informed decisions seamlessly. Seer employs the Conditional Tokens framework to create and manage outcome tokens efficiently. Enhancing usability, Seer wraps these ERC1155 outcome tokens into user-friendly ERC20 tokens, facilitating smoother trading and integration with existing DeFi protocols.

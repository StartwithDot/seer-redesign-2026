# Inspiration notes v3 (homepage, dark EF direction)
Frames: research/inspiration/home3/ (12 sites, 6 scroll frames each; strip-*.png = overview per site). Looked at: Injective, Linear, Stripe Sessions, Monad, Hyperliquid, Phantom, Vercel (Lusion, Igloo, Coinbase, Apple, Uniswap captured, not reviewed in depth).

## Hero object quality
- Injective: pure black, ONE hero object (rim-lit hand holding a glass tile with the logo). Only violet rim light, no glow blobs. Headline sits below the object, overlapping its lower edge slightly. The object is the brand.
- Stripe Sessions: a single huge ribbon form, full colour gradient, crisp edges, sits in a rounded frame under a huge light headline.
- Hyperliquid: sparse white/teal dot field as hero texture, dots of varied size = depth. Proof that dots read as premium when few, crisp and sized with intent.
- Takeaway for Seer: one hero object only = the raven logo as crisp white dots, varied size for depth, violet rim accents. No glows. Headline directly below, overlapping.

## Colour scale
- Injective: #000 page, #4B3BFF-like electric violet for buttons and the big panel, white text, grey #8a8a99 secondary. Cards are #0d0d12 on black with 1px faint border.
- Linear: #08090a, text steps white / 60% / 40%; almost no accent. Hierarchy comes from grey steps.
- Seer scale: #0a0a0a surface, #121218 raised, violet #7B3FF2 (EF) for lines and accents, electric violet panel #4B3BFF-ish only as full-width chapter panels, white 100/65/40 for text.

## Type
- Injective: large serif display (white, tight), serif body, tiny sans nav. Elegant, institutional.
- Linear/Vercel/Stripe: grotesk display, huge, 2 lines max, tight tracking, left aligned with a small muted sub line.
- Seer: Inter Tight for UI and body; serif display only for the single hero headline if it tests better. Two lines max per headline.

## Panel structure
- Injective chapter = one rounded full-width violet panel, left: serif headline + small body, right: tone-on-tone isometric object (dome). Then black sections with centred headline, logo marquee, tabbed cards where the active card turns violet.
- Linear: left-aligned headline + right-aligned small descriptor, product UI shown as real UI, 1px dividers, "Fig 0.1" captions under line-art isometrics.
- Monad: hairline grid frames, mono micro-labels ("USE CASES"), line icons that animate.
- Takeaway: alternate black sections and full-width violet panels; one object per chapter; real UI snippets, not cards of icons.

## Scroll choreography
- Injective, Linear: sections are calm, pinned objects change state, little parallax. Motion is slow and mechanical.
- Hyperliquid: dot field persists across sections as a continuous texture, logo built from dots reappears at the end.
- Seer: the reversed EF line (one in, many out) is the spine. It enters from the left at the hero, fans out at each chapter, the dot raven travels along it and shrinks into the last page.

## How an explanatory animation is staged
- Phantom: the prediction market card shows YES/NO tickets and a payout, i.e. the idea is shown with the real objects, one loop, no text needed.
- Linear: a small chart/agent diagram animates once when the section enters, then rests.
- Rules: one idea per animation, 3 to 5 s loop or scroll-scrubbed, objects labelled with real words (YES, NO, 62%), ends on a readable still frame.

## Avoid (seen in weaker sites)
- Icon card rows, stat strips (Injective by the Numbers is the weakest block), logo walls as filler, carousel arrows.

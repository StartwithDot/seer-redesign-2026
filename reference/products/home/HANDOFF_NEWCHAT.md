## 1. What changed: the strategic frame (from Seer's own X post, saved in products/home/x_post/post.md, read it and view a.png, b.png, c.png)
- Seer is rebranding from "prediction markets" to **Information Finance**: markets as a way to DISCOVER information, not just a place to trade. Tagline: "Information Finance infrastructure for the agentic economy."
- Design for the person ASKING the question as much as the trader. A visitor must learn: who is asking, what the market prices, how to use that signal, without becoming a trader.
- **EF (Electric Foundation) is the starting point**: near-black surfaces, violet. My last light/pastel/multicolour pages (A, B, C in deliver/home2/) DRIFTED from EF and are not what the user wants as a direction. Stay inside EF: near-black + violet, white and violet tints, Inter Tight (+ a serif for display is acceptable, see Injective).
- **EF line motif must be reversed**: ONE line from the LEFT fans out into MANY parallel lines to the RIGHT (one question becomes many outcomes). See products/home/x_post/c.png (violet lines on black, white raven logo + italic SEER wordmark). Use this as the connective tissue of the homepage (it can drive the scroll animation and section transitions).
- Homepage brief: introduce Seer as the protocol beneath the applications and show how they fit in one ecosystem. The five apps and their Information-Finance roles: Seer App = explorer of all markets (Etherscan of Seer); Deep = many-outcome questions incl. markets for AI agents (agents forecast how humans will judge a field of grant proposals); Futarchy = conditional markets, informs a DAO without deciding for it; Opportunity Markets = forecast which submissions get selected, reviewers keep the final say; Risk Pricing = compare market estimates of risk across protocols. Also the agentic economy: what useful features come when agents ask and trade?
- Stats the post uses (usable as honest context, cite source The Block): $331B traded Jan to Sep 2026, 77% sports and parlays, under 2% information.

Colors: white/near-white dots on black, violet accents, full-width violet panels like Injective's second block for the app chapters.
- Quality bar: premium art direction (rendered/illustrated objects with fine detail), not flat stickers. Consider an isometric or crisp vector-illustrated object per app in tone-on-tone violet (like Injective's watch) as the signature of each chapter.

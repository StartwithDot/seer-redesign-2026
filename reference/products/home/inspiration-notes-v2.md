## 10 principles for Seer

1. Ground: warm paper #F6F1E7 as the base, never black. Tinted section grounds: peach #FFD9C2, lilac #DCD3FF, mint #CFF1E0, butter #FFE98A, sky #CFE3FF. Ink #14122B. One loud accent per section (violet #5B3DF5, coral #FF5B3D, lime #B8F26B, cobalt #2F5BFF).
2. Hero: left headline in serif display with one italic accent word on a highlighter pill (Notion/ENS), right a large rounded stage (#DCD3FF) holding the particle mark. Particles are ink #14122B on lilac, 2px crisp dots, no blur, no glow.
3. Hero product proof: a real market card (question, YES/NO prices, small odds sparkline) overlaps the stage edge, tilted 2 degrees, like Gumroad stickers. One CTA pill, one secondary text link.
4. Color blocking, not mesh: every section is a full-width flat color field with a rounded 32px top edge overlapping the previous section. Stripe-style ribbon only as one flat three-color shape in the hero background.
5. Five apps without a card grid: a horizontal sequence of five full-width color chapters (one hue each: Futarchy violet, Risk coral, Opportunity lime, Deep cobalt, Explorer butter). Each chapter pairs one cropped real UI on one side with one sentence and one verb on the other, alternating sides. A sticky five-dot index tracks the chapter.
6. Mascot: the particle bird (the brand mark) detaches from the hero stage and flies down the page. At each chapter it perches on the UI edge and takes that chapter's hue, then drifts to the next. Path is a single smooth curve, bird stays 64 to 96px, crisp ink silhouette on all grounds.
7. Scroll choreography: pin the hero for about one viewport while particles resolve from scattered dots into the mark, then release the bird on scroll. Chapters slide up over each other (stacked sticky cards with rounded tops). No parallax text, no counters counting up.
8. Type: serif display (e.g. Instrument Serif or Fraunces) at 96 to 140px for hero and chapter titles, a clean grotesk (Inter Tight or General Sans) for body, tabular mono only inside product UI. Max 12 words per headline.
9. Texture of humanity: sticker objects with 2px ink outline and flat offset shadow (Gumroad), tiny handwritten annotation arrows on product shots (Ramp), market-tag chips in tinted fills (ENS). Use sparingly, 1 to 2 per section.
10. Rhythm and brevity: seven sections max: hero, one-line what is Seer, five app chapters, how a market resolves (three steps on a lilac field), trust strip in butter, closing CTA on a full coral or violet field with the bird landing on the button. Footer on ink with paper text is the only dark area.

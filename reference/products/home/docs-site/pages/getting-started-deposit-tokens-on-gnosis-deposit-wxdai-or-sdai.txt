Most methods of obtaining wxDAI or sDAI require users to have xDAI first. ​ Wrap xDAI Wrap your xDai in Wrap Eth connected with MetaMask to Gnosis . ​ Interact with the respective smart contracts Another way to convert xDAI to wxDAI or sDAI is to interact directly with their respective smart contracts on Gnosis Block Explorer . ​ wxDAI Block Explorer Url: https://gnosisscan.io/address/0xe91D153E0b41518A2Ce8Dd3D7944Fa863463a97d Visit the url, select Contract -&gt; Write Contract . Click Connect to Web3 to connect your wallet. Make sure the network is Gnosis . Under deposit , input the amount of xDAI you want to convert. ​ sDAI Block Explorer Url: https://gnosisscan.io/address/0xD499b51fcFc66bd31248ef4b28d656d67E591A94 Visit the url, select Contract -&gt; Write Contract . Click Connect to Web3 to connect your wallet. Make sure the network is Gnosis . Under depositXDAI , input the amount of xDAI you want to convert and the receiver address (you can paste your wallet address here). ​ Swap from other tokens You can swap to wxDAI or sDAI on decentralized exchanges.

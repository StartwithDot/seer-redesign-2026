In the absence of subsidies or a significant trading volume allowing them to profit from the spread, providing liquidity is not profitable due to the impermanent loss (see Lack of Liquidity section). Information seekers cannot get accurate estimates as markets are only efficient if there are a significant amount of actors participating. They can’t convince other actors either if the markets are not widely used.

Metamask is a widely used one. Head to https://metamask.io/download/ to download and install it. ​ Connect your wallet to Seer On the right side of the navigation bar, you can see a Connect button. Click it to connect your wallet to Seer. In addition to Metamask, you can use other wallets of your choice. After you have connected, the Connect button will be replaced by a button showing the current network and your wallet address. ​ Switch network Users can choose to interact with Seer on either Ethereum Mainnet or Gnosis . Each network features its own set of prediction markets and utilizes different tokens. To switch to a different network, click on the network icon.

Before creating your market, please read the Verified Markets Policy and Market Resolution Policy . On the home page, click Create a market . Select a market type: Input the question and outcomes: In the case of multi-scalar market , question name should follow a specific format. Since each outcome have its own question, you should place the outcome type in brackets to replace the outcome value in the question. For example: How many electoral votes will the [party name] win in the 2024 U.S. Presidential Election? Specifying an opening date. The market can only be resolved after the opening date: Preview the market: After previewing, click Create Market . You can verify the market during creation by clicking the switch button in the Verify it Now row. If you prefer, you can also complete the verification later. Unverified market will display a warning. Please refer to the next section to learn how to verify a market.

## Math module contracts (src/math)
- rng.ts: mulberry32(seed) + d3-random normal via randomNormal.source(rng).
- gbm.ts: step(S, mu, sigma, dt, z) = S*exp((mu - sigma^2/2)dt + sigma*sqrt(dt)*z).
- ou.ts: step(g, kappa, theta, eta, dt, z) exact discretisation: g' = theta + (g-theta)e^{-kappa dt} + eta*sqrt((1-e^{-2 kappa dt})/(2 kappa))*z. stationary variance s2 = eta^2/(2 kappa). E[g(t)|g0] = theta + (g0-theta)e^{-kappa(t-now)}.
- series.ts: buildSeries(seed, params) -> arrays over a timeline in DAYS: t0=-14, fork=-8 (market creation), now=0, T=+6.62 (decision), step 1/48 day. Spot S via GBM (annualised sigma 0.55, mu 0, S(now)=2432.83 by shifting). For t>=fork: P_yes = S(1+g/2), P_no = S(1-g/2), g from OU (kappa 3/day, theta 0.0004, eta 0.004 so gap stays tiny/inside band like the live market) with g(now)=+0.0000. Provide a second preset theta 0.018 (clear Include) and -0.016 (Exclude) to demo verdict flips via a hidden dev control: key "v" cycles presets.
- twap.ts: window=[T-5, T]. twapGap(now) = (1/5) * integral over window of g: use realised g for t<=now and E[g(t)|g(now)] for t>now. Cone: Var of final TWAP given info at now = Var_rem/25, where Var_rem = (2*s2/kappa^2) * (kappa*L - (1 - e^{-kappa L})) for L = length in days of the unobserved part of the window (0 once now>=T), and the cone is +-1.5*sqrt(Var_rem)/5 around the running estimate; it narrows to zero at T. Verdict(x): x>0.01 Include, x<-0.01 Exclude, else Neutral.

## ForkChart (canvas 2D, the signature, src/components/ForkChart.tsx)
Props: series, nowIndex, mode 'hero' | 'detail', onHover. Two panes on one shared x-axis (days): PRICE pane (78% height) and GAP pane (22%, detail mode only).
PRICE pane: horizontal hairlines at 3-4 nice ticks (var --line), labels in --text-3 11px tabular. Shared history (t0..fork) in --text-2 1.5px. From fork to now: NO thread --text-2 (#a8a8b3) 2px, YES thread --violet 2px. After now: threads continue as 1px dashed projections of E[price] (spot-based) that fade (alpha 0.6->0.1) and fan with a diffusion cone (sqrt(t) shape) filled rgba(violet,.06). Decision-band CORRIDOR: polygon NO*(1+-0.01) from fork to T, fill rgba(123,58,255,.10), edges 1px rgba(156,111,255,.35). Where YES lies above the top edge fill that region violet .22; below the bottom edge fill red .18. Fork point: a 5px ring marker + vertical hairline. NOW: vertical line with a pill label "now" removed (no words): use a small filled dot on each thread and a vertical hairline. TWAP window slab: rect [T-5,T] fill rgba(255,255,255,.03), left/right 1px edges, with tiny ticks. Decision time T: solid 1px line + a lock-open glyph that turns to a lock when now>=T.
GAP pane: g(t) in % (YES-NO)/NO. Dashed lines at +1% and -1% in --text-3; zero line solid --line. g(t) line --violet 1.5px for t<=now. Inside the slab: running TWAP of g as a rising AREA (fill violet .28 if > +1%, red .24 if < -1%, grey .12 otherwise) with its line 2px; the projection cone (see twap.ts) as translucent wedge from now to T pinching to a point at T. A verdict dot at T on the running-TWAP line.
Interactions: hover shows vertical crosshair + tooltip (time, YES, NO, Impact; numbers only). Scrubber: a thin range input under the chart controlling `now` between fork+1d and T; play/pause button (space) advances now at 1 day/second then stops at T; at T the verdict locks (haptic pulse on gauge). `now` drag recomputes everything with spring-eased cone morph (motion value, critically damped). Arrow keys scrub when the chart is focused.
Motion: on mount draw-in 900ms ease-out (clip rect reveal left-to-right, threads separate at the fork with a spring over 600ms starting when the reveal passes the fork). Live tick every 2.5s in 'detail' when now is at the real now: append a point, numbers roll. DPR cap 2, ResizeObserver, redraw via rAF only when dirty or animating; pause when off-screen (IntersectionObserver); prefers-reduced-motion: no draw-in, no tick, no play.
Hero mode: no axes/labels/gap pane, lower opacity, edge-to-edge 100vw behind overlay, slow parallax-free; still shows fork, corridor, slab.

## What it is (thesis + world)
Thesis: the market answers one question in two parallel worlds; the gap is the verdict. World: **"The Fork"**: shared history forks at market creation into a YES thread (violet) and a NO thread (ink-grey); a +-1% corridor is the decision rule; a TWAP slab and a rising gap area decide; a uncertainty cone pinches toward the decision. Real model (live futarchy.seer.pm): milestones = conditional token PRICE markets: "What will the impact on {TOKEN} price be if {PROPOSAL}?", YES Price / NO Price in USDS, Impact %, 5-day TWAP, rule: include if YES > NO by more than 1%, exclude if NO > YES by more than 1%, neutral inside. Math: GBM spot (src/math/gbm.ts), Ornstein-Uhlenbeck gap (ou.ts), TWAP integral + remaining-variance cone (twap.ts), seeded RNG (rng.ts), simplex noise (noise.ts) for the Field view.

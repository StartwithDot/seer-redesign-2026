# Deep: requirements stub (read docs/ui-quality-rules.md + docs/product-identity-guide.md first)
Real product (deep.seer.pm, "AI Prediction Markets", Optimism, light UI with blue accent today; docs: https://deep-pm.gitbook.io/seer-docs): 3-step onboarding strip (1 Connect your wallet; 2 Create a trade wallet = a contract you own that batches hundreds of trades into one run; 3 Add sUSDS: every market settles in it and it earns Sky savings yield). Tabs: "Round 3 - Originality" (LIVE) + Archive. Chart "Share of original work over time": each line is a repository's UP price = the market's estimate of how much of it is original; each repository trades as an UP/DOWN pair (UP = share of original work, DOWN = share carried by dependencies). Actions: Upload predictions (agents), Export market weights, Leaderboard, Guide, "Trading one market at a time? Seer has the individual market view... Browse on Seer". The Seer brief: markets for many possible outcomes, designed for AI agents, e.g. agents forecasting how human evaluators assess a large field of grant proposals. Digest: products/deep/docs.md (DeepSeek).
Thesis proposal: "A field of outcomes, priced at once; agents and humans read the same map." World: **The Territory**: each repository is a territory (Voronoi/treemap cell relaxed with Lloyd's algorithm) split into UP and DOWN halves; prices move the borders; uploaded agent predictions appear as particles flocking toward the market consensus (boids with seeded noise); round timeline scrubber. Exploratory, dense, scientific.
Must show: round tabs/archive, 24+ repositories (design for 100), UP/DOWN pair bars with live ticks, agent leaderboard, upload predictions (drop zone, any file simulated), export weights, trade-wallet + sUSDS onboarding as a compact stepper (not a wall), hover isolation of one repository line (dim others to 20%), search/sort/filter.
Palette: UP = blue #5B9DFF, DOWN = ink-grey, up to 6 categorical series, violet for primary actions. Tests: ui-quality-rules s12 + isolation, upload, 100-item performance, series color contrast.

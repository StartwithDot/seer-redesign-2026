Markdown versions of documentation pages are available by appending `.md` to page URLs; this page is available as [Markdown](https://deep-pm.gitbook.io/seer-docs/trading-on-deep-seer/step-by-step-guide.md). # Step-by-step guide #### Step 1: Upload Predictions Trading is driven by your predicted prices. 1. Click Upload Predictions 2. Upload a .csv file with your predictions 3. You can download and edit the provided sample CSV 4. Click Load Predictions #### Step 2: Review Market Data After uploading, you’ll see a table with: * Current market prices * Your predicted prices * Your token balances * Price differences (market vs prediction) #### Step 3: Execute Strategy Click Start Trading to run the automated strategy. * Enter your starting sUSDS amount * Click Execute Strategy * Trades will be executed automatically

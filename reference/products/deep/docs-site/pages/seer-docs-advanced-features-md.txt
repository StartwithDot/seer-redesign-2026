Markdown versions of documentation pages are available by appending `.md` to page URLs; this page is available as [Markdown](https://deep-pm.gitbook.io/seer-docs/advanced-features.md). # ⚙️ Advanced Features #### Sell All to sUSDS The Sell all to sUSDS button: * Attempts to convert all tokens back to sUSDS * Executes multiple sell transactions ⚠️ Notes: * Liquidity may prevent full conversion * You may receive less than originally spent * Gas costs can be high due to multiple transactions * For a more efficient approach, see the Rebalancing Strategy section below *** ### Rebalancing Strategy (Recommended) If you want to update your predictions: 1. Upload a new CSV 2. Click Start Trading 3. Leave the sUSDS input empty Deep Seer will: * Use your existing token balances * Automatically rebalance your portfolio ✅ This is more efficient than selling everything and starting over

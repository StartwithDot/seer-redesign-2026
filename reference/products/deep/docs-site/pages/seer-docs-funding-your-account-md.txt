Markdown versions of documentation pages are available by appending `.md` to page URLs; this page is available as [Markdown](https://deep-pm.gitbook.io/seer-docs/funding-your-account.md). # 💰 Funding Your Account #### Supported Token: sUSDS All prediction markets are traded using sUSDS on Optimism. 💡 **Savings USDS (often written as sUSDS)** is a yield-bearing version of USDS, designed to automatically earn interest while you hold it. You can still transfer it like a normal token and, on supported DeFi protocols, use it for activities such as staking or lending. If you don’t have sUSDS, you can convert directly on the site. #### Convert Tokens to sUSDS 1. Click Deposit sUSDS 2. Select Convert USDC/USDS to sUSDS 3. Enter the amount 4. Click Supply To convert back: * Go to the Withdraw tab * Convert sUSDS → USDC or USDS #### Deposit to Trade Wallet Once you have sUSDS: 1. Click Deposit sUSDS 2. Enter amount 3. Click Deposit To withdraw: * Use Withdraw sUSDS to move funds back to your main wallet

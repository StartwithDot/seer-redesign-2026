Copy On this page 📊 Trading on Deep Seer Example: L2 Strategy The L2 market consists of : A parent market Multiple child markets (derived from parent outcomes) Deep Seer : Interacts across these related markets Buys/sells tokens to optimize your position based on your predictions 📌 Worked Example Suppose INPUT = 1,000 sUSD 2 repos: A, B. A has 2 dependencies: A1, A2. B has 3 dependencies: B1, B2, B3 Step 1: Mint complete sets 1000sUSDS → 1000A, 1000B → 1000A1, 1000A2, 1000B1, 1000B2, 1000B3 Repo Dependencies Current weight Predicted weight Balance before mint Balance after mint A A1 0.1 0.15 500 1500 A A2 0.2 0.1 300 1300 B B1 0.15 0.05 100 1100 B B2 0.4 0.6 400 1400 B B3 0.3 0.2 150 1150 Classification Over-priced (sell): A2, B1, B3 Under-priced (buy): A1, B2 Step 2: Process for each multiscalar market Sell overvalued outcomes: Dependencies Weight before sell Predicted weight Average price Balance before sell Balance after sell Collateral after sell A2 0.2 0.1 0.15 1300 300 150 B1 0.15 0.05 0.1 1100 400 70 B3 0.3 0.2 0.25 1150 350 200 Redeem complete sets: Dependencies Balance before redeem Balance after redeem Collateral before redeem Collateral after redeem A1 1500 1200 150 450 A2 300 0 B1 400 50 270 620 B2 1400 1050 B3 350 0 Buy undervalued outcomes: Dependencies Current weight Predicted weight Average price Allocated L1 Balance before buy Balance after buy A1 0.1 0.15 0.12 450 1200 4950 B2 0.4 0.6 0.52 620 1050 2242.3 Result: End up holding mostly undervalued tokens, with few or no overvalued tokens. 🔄 After Trading Your updated balances will appear in the table Tokens remain in your trade wallet, not your main wallet Previous Step-by-step guide Next ⚙️ Advanced Features Last updated 6 months ago

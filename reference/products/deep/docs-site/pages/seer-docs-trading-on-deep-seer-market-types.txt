Copy On this page 📊 Trading on Deep Seer Market types There are 3 market sets: L1 : a multiscalar market where each outcome token (each outcome token represents a specific option of the prediction market f.e ARTGOTORGSOL token represents argotorg/solidity) will redeem for the proportion of the contribution of the repository to Ethereum according to the estimates given by a panel of expert jurors. Originality : a conditional scalar market with UP and DOWN token that sum up to 1, meant to evaluate the percentage of dependencies contributions to each repository on the condition that the repository is picked for originality evaluation. L2 : similarly to the first market, each outcome token will redeem for the proportion of the contribution of the dependencies to L1 repositories according to the estimates given by a panel of expert jurors making it a multiscalar market, however, this market is conditional on which of the repositories will have their dependencies evaluated.

Copy On this page 💰 Funding Your Account Supported Token: sUSDS All prediction markets are traded using sUSDS on Optimism. 💡 Savings USDS (often written as sUSDS) is a yield-bearing version of USDS, designed to automatically earn interest while you hold it. You can still transfer it like a normal token and, on supported DeFi protocols, use it for activities such as staking or lending. If you don’t have sUSDS, you can convert directly on the site.

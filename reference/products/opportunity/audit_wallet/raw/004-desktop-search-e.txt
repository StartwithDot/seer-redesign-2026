Seer AI Job Board Name

Seer is experimenting with a futarchy-run organization that will build a job board for AI: humans and AIs post tasks, AI completes them and gets paid in crypto. Step one is picking the name.

Every name suggested by the community is listed as an outcome. Traders buy shares in the name they believe will be adopted.

The chosen name earns $1,000 in crypto (if Seer can use it). By suggesting a name, its author transfers all rights to it, effective only once the $1,000 is paid. If several people suggested the same name, the earliest reply wins.

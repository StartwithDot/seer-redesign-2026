This market is used to choose which visual identity Seer will select for its rebrand.

Every submission is listed as an outcome of this multi-scalar market. Traders buy shares in the direction they believe the team will pick. Final selection stays with the team, with market prices as the community signal.

The winning entry receives $3,000. The top 5 submissions by price also receive Seer credits.

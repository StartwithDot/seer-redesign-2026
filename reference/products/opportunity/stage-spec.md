# Opportunity Markets "Stage": build spec (binding; the user approved the Stage preview and gave these changes)
Visual baseline: products/opportunity/concepts-preview/stage.html (open it, port its look and behavior; do NOT redesign what the user liked: EF dark violet, left Markets rail, treemap stage, step bar, right submission panel, simulated wallet). Data: products/opportunity/data/*.json (markets.json = 3 REAL markets; live_outcomes.json 235 rows; finalized_outcomes.json 17; devcon_outcomes.json 18). Build in apps/opportunity (Vite + React 19 + TS strict, plain CSS + packages/ef/tokens.css, `motion` for springs/layout; no other libs). Reuse from the existing apps/opportunity where good (router, HoverMenu, Tooltip, RollingNumber, CommandPalette, WalletDialog, Avatar, theme toggle, e2e harness) and DELETE the old Landing/Market/Board/CutChart/PriceHistory pages. Rules: docs/ui-quality-rules.md and docs/coding-rules.md still apply (fixed sizes, no overlaps, label engine, no AI-slop: no KPI-tile strips, no gradient text, no glass, no glow, no emoji, no stock icons).

## Hard constraints from the user
1. Every route fits 1440x900 with NO page scroll (document height == viewport). Only bounded inner panes scroll (scrollbar-gutter, edge fade). Nothing is vacant: no unlabeled empty tiles/boxes anywhere.
2. Many markets are first-class: 3 real + up to 9 deterministic SAMPLE markets (tag 'Sample', footer chip 'Sample data'; `?n=1..12`). Selecting a market changes the stage INSTANTLY (animated morph, <= 420ms).
3. Real names only: market names (Seer AI Job Board Name, Seer Rebrand Contest, Seer Merch for Devcon Mumbai), Seer's labels (Opportunity Markets, The pilot, The protocol, Archive, Liquidity, Volume, Outcomes, Odds, Price, Buy/Sell, Connect wallet, Open opportunity, Past markets, Finalized, Sponsor, Review) and the live site's 5-step chain TEXT exactly: "Call for entries become submissions" -> "Traders set the odds of selection" -> "Price becomes a triage tool" -> "Sponsor makes final decision" -> "Traders earn P&L based on final decision". Headline subtitle: "Opportunity Markets turn any selection process into a market." Roadmap labels: TODAY Seer One operates it / NEXT Anyone launches / ANY DECISION One shared layer.
4. Zero dead controls (a click-all e2e test asserts every visible control changes URL, DOM, dialog, focus or is aria-disabled with a tooltip).

Selected row highlighted; row click selects; keyboard arrows.
- CENTER STAGE card: header (status tag, name, Volume/Liquidity/Outcomes/decision type, subtitle line), view tabs [Field | List] (segmented, animated thumb), the FIELD (below), review-cut slider row with label and live readout 'Top 27 cover 60.4% of odds', sponsor lane row (amber lock 'Sponsor decision: pick 1' or 'pick 5', status Pending / Winner), and at the bottom the STEP BAR.
- RIGHT PANEL: 'Selected submission' (avatar, name, Odds %, Price sDAI, Rank, mini price history 96px high with labelled min/max and Today/14 days ago, 'Review submission' opens the lot drawer) and the COMPACT TICKET.

## FIELD (replaces the treemap with vacant boxes)
Area = odds, but nothing unlabeled: pick N = the largest set of top tiles that can each show name + odds % at min 112x64px (typically 10-16); render them as labeled tiles (name 14px/500 ellipsis + tooltip, odds 12px, leader tile bigger with rank). All remaining priced submissions merge into ONE labeled block 'N more priced . X% of odds' (click = switch to List tab filtered to them). The floor tier is ONE labeled band 'Tied at 0.4% . 46' and unpriced is ONE hatched band 'No pool . 130' (click = List tab filtered). Legend + tier counts above the field double as filters. Finalized market: Winner tile outlined amber with 'Winner . pays 1.00 sDAI'; Top-5 markets mark five winners. Odds ticks (sample feed every 1.5-2.5s) flash tiles (green/red 600ms) and re-layout with springs; the leader tile breathes subtly (scale 1.00-1.012, 4s). Market switch: tiles morph (FLIP) to the new layout. LIST tab = ranked table in a bounded pane (Rank, Submission, Odds %, Price sDAI, 24h, Position) with tier filter chips and the cut row; row click selects (updates right panel + highlights the tile), double-click opens the drawer.
Review cut slider moves k: top-k tiles get a bright violet outline and a 'Review' tag; others dim to 60%. Sponsor lane never colored violet; amber is ONLY sponsor.

## STEP BAR (the product's own 5 steps, animated, labelled with their text; also drives the Field)
5 equal segments with numbers 01-05 and the exact texts above (12-13px, 2 lines max). Autoplay (3.6s per step, play/pause button, click any step). Their colors: chips use the live site's purples: idle #110d1c fill with #A774D1 text at 70%; active step fill #520078 with #A774D1 text/border; steps 3 and 5 (Price becomes a triage tool, Traders earn P&L) carry a lavender #A774D1 underline accent as on opportunity.seer.pm. Animation: a signal pulse (small bright dot + soft ring, CSS @property-driven) travels along the connector from step to step; the active step's ring blinks once per second; the field reacts per step: 01 equal-area cells labelled with count 'N submissions', 02 sized by odds, 03 cut slider sweeps to k and dims the rest, 04 shortlist lifts into the amber sponsor lane, 05 payout replay (winner tile expands, others fade; for active markets show 'Payout 1.00 sDAI per winning share' as a note). Reduced motion = no autoplay, no pulse.

## MARQUEE (top strip, same page)

Click selects the market (stage morph). When a tick happens the chip flashes. Respect reduced motion (static, horizontally scrollable). It must not overlap the header or rail.

## TOUR ('?' button; auto-start first visit only)
Blurred focus experience: a full-viewport scrim with backdrop-filter: blur(10px) + dim, with a rounded cut-out around the target that stays sharp (spotlight; implement with an overlay div + clip-path/mask and a ring that animates between targets with a spring). The step card sits beside the target, never over it, 12 words max, with Back/Next/Skip and 'n / 8'. NOTHING from the page may show through in the middle (the old overlay let market names bleed over the headline: that is the bug to avoid). Steps: 1 Markets list 2 Marquee 3 Field 4 Review cut 5 Sponsor decision 6 Step bar 7 Ticket 8 Connect wallet. Focus trapped, Esc closes, reduced motion = no blur animation.

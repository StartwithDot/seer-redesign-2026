# Opportunity Markets: requirements stub (read docs/ui-quality-rules.md + docs/product-identity-guide.md first)
Real product (opportunity.seer.pm, observed): headline "With AI, the cost of reviewing work is more than the time it takes to do it". Pitch: turn any selection process into a market; traders back the submission they think will be selected, odds move, sponsors prioritise review on top-rated submissions. Flow: Call for entries become submissions -> Traders set the odds of selection -> Price becomes a triage tool -> Sponsor makes the final decision -> Traders earn P&L on the final decision. Pilot: one live market "Seer AI Job Board Name" (ACTIVE, liquidity $2.36K; leaders delegateyourjob.ai 5.9%, Zetsu.AI 4.1%, Wageware 3.6%, +232 more; "OPEN OPPORTUNITY"); archive of 2 past markets (rebrand, Devcon merch). Protocol roadmap: TODAY Seer One operates it, NEXT anyone launches, ANY DECISION one shared layer. Digest: products/opportunity/docs.md (DeepSeek, if present).
Thesis proposal: "Odds are a triage queue; the sponsor still decides." World: **The Sieve**: ~235 submissions fall through a Galton board whose peg bias follows the odds; the top few collect in a review lane (FLIP reorder as odds tick); the SPONSOR decision lane is separate, locked until the decision (padlock, amber/gold authority color vs violet market color). Math: binomial -> normal (Galton), softmax of odds, rank stability. Kinetic, fast (springs), competitive board feel; hero = ranked board + sieve strip, NOT Futarchy's list+chart.
Must show: submissions (name, odds %, delta, depth), market vs sponsor authority separation, P&L logic hint (tooltip), archive, "Open opportunity", create/launch affordance ("Anyone launches" next). Scale: 1 live market now but design for 1..12 markets and 235+ submissions (virtualized list, search, filters).
Palette: violet = market odds, amber = sponsor decision, ink-grey neutral, green/red deltas only. Tests: ui-quality-rules s12 + odds sum consistency, lane separation, FLIP no jump, 235-row performance.

# risk: documentation digest (DeepSeek, quote-verified)

## What it is
- Foresight is presented within Kleros Help, with the brand line “See Further. Decide Smarter.” [https://foresight.kleros.io]
- Foresight frames its purpose as predicting trends and informing decisions. [https://foresight.kleros.io]
- A risk interface is branded “Foresight | Kleros” and includes Guide, Connect, Risk Pricing, and Predictions. [https://risk.seer.pm]
- Risk Pricing asks users to predict the yearly Probability of Default for DeFi assets during Q3 2026 and to estimate the risk level of listed protocols. [https://risk.seer.pm]
- Seer is the prediction market platform described as built on Reality.eth and the Conditional Tokens Framework; it supports specialized market types beyond standard markets, including conditional and futarchy markets. [https://docs.seer.pm/llms.txt]

## Who uses it and why
- The facts identify “users” who set estimates for displayed protocols, choose how many protocols to predict on, use CSV upload for predictions, and take the action to predict all entered estimates above. [https://risk.seer.pm]
- Users can check opportunities to LP or trade specific outcome tokens in Seer. [https://risk.seer.pm]
- Users can download the latest data, updated in the last 24 hours, in CSV format. [https://risk.seer.pm]
- The Seer solution defines market roles and economics via prediction market actors, information seekers, token incentives, and safe liquidity provision. [https://docs.seer.pm/llms.txt]
- The Seer app includes a leaderboard and Trader Score for user performance tracking. [https://docs.seer.pm/llms.txt]
- Foresight’s stated purpose is predicting trends and informing decisions; Risk Pricing asks for Probability of Default and risk level estimates. [https://foresight.kleros.io] [https://risk.seer.pm]

## Core mechanism (step by step)
Documented flow elements; the facts do not provide a complete ordered sequence.

1. Entry/UI: Foresight includes an “EXPLORE AVAILABLE PREDICTIONS” section; Foresight | Kleros includes Guide, Connect, Risk Pricing, and Predictions. [https://foresight.kleros.io] [https://risk.seer.pm]
2. Risk prompt: users are asked to predict the yearly Probability of Default for DeFi assets during Q3 2026 and estimate the risk level of listed protocols. [https://risk.seer.pm]
3. Input: users set estimates for the protocols displayed below; users can choose how many protocols they want to predict on; the same capital can be used to predict on all protocols at once. [https://risk.seer.pm]
4. Submission aids: the interface includes CSV upload for predictions, an action to predict all entered estimates above, and Connect To Predict and Advanced actions. [https://risk.seer.pm]
5. Timing: the trading period runs until Wednesday, 30 September 2026 at 23:59 UTC; Q3 2026 is presented alongside Q4 2026, which is marked coming soon. [https://risk.seer.pm]
6. Adjacent market access: users can check opportunities to LP or trade specific outcome tokens in Seer, and can download the latest data, updated in the last 24 hours, as CSV. [https://risk.seer.pm]
7. Seer mechanics: collateral is deposited per network and asset, including DAI and sDAI on Ethereum and xDAI or wxDAI/sDAI on Gnosis; outcome tokens can be minted, merged, and redeemed. Trading is a distinct app flow for buying and selling outcome tokens; liquidity provision is a first-class app function separate from trading. [https://docs.seer.pm/llms.txt]
8. Seer design/economics: the whitepaper attributes prior prediction market failures to causes including lack of liquidity and regulatory attacks; Seer’s proposed practical implementation combines an AMM with auctions; the solution defines market roles and economics via prediction market actors, information seekers, token incentives, and safe liquidity provision. [https://docs.seer.pm/llms.txt]

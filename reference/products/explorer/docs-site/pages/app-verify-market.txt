It provides extra credibility to the market being created. Verified markets must meet strict criteria to protect users. For more details, please refer to the Verified Markets Policy . Additionally, verification requires the submission of images for both the market and its outcomes. You can choose to verify your market either at or after its creation. This popup will appear if you click the switch button on Verify it Now when creating a new market Or: This is your market card on the home page Please note that you need to have sufficient xDAI available to verify a market. The deposited xDAI will be returned once your market is verified: Verified market will be shown with images and a Verified tag: Verifying markets you submitted will also be displayed with images, but these images will only be visible to you.

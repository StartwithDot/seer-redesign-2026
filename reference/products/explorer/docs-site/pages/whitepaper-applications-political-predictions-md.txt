> ## Documentation Index > Fetch the complete documentation index at: https://docs.seer.pm/llms.txt > Use this file to discover all available pages before exploring further. # Political Predictions Predicting electoral outcomes has historically been the most successful usecase of prediction markets. Indeed, as those markets are the simplest to implement (results are known on a well defined date), are high impact (knowing who would rule a country is an important information for individuals and businesses) and draw large attention (as those elections draw attention of voters). Markets on US elections would be the easiest to set up in both technical terms and in terms of gathering public interest.

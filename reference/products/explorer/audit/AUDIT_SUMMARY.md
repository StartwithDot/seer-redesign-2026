# AUDIT_SUMMARY.md

**What it is.** Seer Explorer is the web client for Seer's prediction markets: browse and filter markets, inspect outcome pricing, trade, provide liquidity, and track trader performance. It is explicitly beta (persistent banner in 001) and multi-chain — Filters offer Ethereum, OP Mainnet, Gnosis, Base (006) — with balances shown in xDAI/sDAI (account button “24.69 xDAI”, 001). Data comes from `.netlify/functions/markets-search` (2947 markets, 100/page), each record carrying outcomes, odds, liquidity/volume/open-interest, questions, verification and optional `parentMarket`/`parentOutcome`.

**Screens.** *Home (001):* purple header (Markets, Leaderboard, Create Market, Policies, App, chain, wallet, account, help, theme), beta banner, search (`?marketName=`), Filters dropdown (006), “Create New Market”, “My collections”. Markets render as a 4-column equal-tile grid: title+avatar, outcome donut with 2–3 legend rows, footer with SEER logo, liquidity (`$27.74k`) and tiny status icons; infinite scroll, no table or sort header. Card hover gives an “Open interest and liquidity breakdown” mixing units (007). *Market detail (037):* breadcrumb, colour-coded status strip (“Reports open”, “Trading Open”, “Pending execution”), favourite star, question, “Answer on Reality.eth!” opening the Report Answer dialog (bond 10 xDAI, 043), meta row with type, liquidity (+ help 051), volume, P&L, Verified; trade ticket; chart (045, 055); outcome rows with radio plus Add Liquidity / View pool details / New conditional market links; “Major Events”; tabs Comments, Related Conditional Markets (051), Top Holders (052), Activity (053); “More” accordion with Mint/Merge/Redeem. Scalar adds a headline estimate and DOWN/UP (audit-types 001); multi-scalar shows a question list with “Answered too soon | Reopen question” and Hide questions (011). Mobile collapses to a hamburger, full-width chart and a floating trade FAB opening a bottom-sheet ticket (m1, scenario 07). *Leaderboard (008, 009, 018):* long Trader Score explainer (0–100, “How it works” link), period 1D/1W/1M/ALL, App/Chain filters, Your Rank, search, sortable table with score badges (78.6 GREAT … N/A), pagination. *Create Market (027):* step 1 of 2, empty “Type” select, disabled Next, Kleros footer. *Collections (032):* sign-in required, empty state. *Menus:* Policies lists two policy links (002); App lists sister apps Futarchy, Deepfunding, Foresight, Opportunity Markets (003); Account lists Account, Deposit, Portfolio, Market Collections, Trade Collateral, “Use smart account” (004).

**Biggest comprehension problems**

1. **Chart y-axis auto-scales, exaggerating flat markets** — 48–52 for a 50/50 market, 8.5–9.2 for a scalar — and end-label pills collide with axis ticks (pill covers “80.00”); x-axis labels are irregular and a black square artifact sits at the origin (045, 055).
2. **The chart plots liquidity-pool implied price, not trades.** The only disclosure is buried in the help tooltip and hover tooltip (“may not fully align with the outcome odds”) (051, 045).
3. **Home cannot be compared or sorted.** Equal-tile cards with 2–3 legend rows, no table, no sort header, no compare view; the only ordering control is inside Filters → Sort By (001, 006, 007).
4. **Unit soup.** Card liquidity in `$`, hover breakdown mixing sDAI, `$` and per-outcome tokens (4.94k YASHAR / 12.15k sDAI), detail metrics with mixed or missing units (001, 007, 037, scenario 01).
5. **Trade ticket semantics.** The order-type dropdown is labelled “Market”; “You will get” renders unrounded (1.1868081659397476); the CTA cycles “Enter an amount” → “Not enough balance.”; “More” hides Mint/Merge/Redeem (scenario 01, 06-*).
6. **“Invalid” outcome unexplained** — “210.85 SER-INVALID” at 0%, no settlement meaning given (037, scenario 01).
7. **Outcome rows are both selection controls and link hosts.** A radio, three inline links and a ticket-changing click compete for the same row (scenario 01, 044).
8. **Conditional markets are never explained.** The only in-product trace is a “Show only conditional markets” filter and a Related Conditional Markets tab; parent/parentOutcome are visible only in API data (006, 051).
9. **Create Market taxonomy and gating.** Categorical/Scalar/Multi Scalar are undescribed, and Next stays disabled until a type is chosen, with no explanation of what each type implies (027).
10. **Leaderboard score is hard to operationalise.** A dense paragraph defines Trader Score; badges include N/A; accounts are generated names (`scientific-decisive-toucan`) or ENS; “How it works” content was not captured (008, 009, 018).

**Unobserved / unknowable.** Wallet-connected balances; trade confirm/sign and approve flow; post-trade state; create-market steps 2+; dispute/Kleros screens; resolve/redeem after close; Portfolio and Account pages; Collections after sign-in; comment posting; add/remove-liquidity transaction UI; policy page body (menu links only); keyboard behaviour; loading/error states beyond skeletons.

**Known gaps resolved.** Scenario 01 (desktop ticket) and scenario 07 (mobile bottom-sheet ticket) now pass 6/6; scenario 04’s pool-details state is captured (03-*): Pool Id `0xb328…30b1`, liquidity chart, SHARES balance ladder. The recurring console 404 is `GET rpc.walletconnect.org/v1/profile/reverse/<addr>` — a WalletConnect ENS reverse-profile lookup for the mock address, **not** a Seer resource. The conditional-market example is confirmed in API data: “Will the National Rally’s candidate win the next French presidential election?” has two child markets (`…election`, `…election-2`) sharing parent `0xe179…` with `parentOutcome` 0 (Yes) and 1 (No); 17 of 100 sampled markets are conditional, alongside WTI >$150 and Iranian-regime-collapse cases.

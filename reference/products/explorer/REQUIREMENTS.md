# Seer app / Explorer: requirements stub (read docs/ui-quality-rules.md + docs/product-identity-guide.md first)
Real product (app.seer.pm, observed): top nav Markets, Leaderboard, Create Market, Policies v, App v, Connect, help, theme; big search ("Search by market, outcome or collection"), Filters dropdown, "Create New Market". Grid of cards: 40px circular logo + question; DONUT chart with legend rows (label, %); footer: Seer mark, volume ($27.74k), small status icons (verified, chain, collection), star/bookmark. Market detail: breadcrumb, status strip ("Reports open"), question, "Answer on Reality.eth", meta row (type Categorical, Liquidity, Volume, Verified), range tabs 1D 1W 1M All Custom, step-line chart with colored end-label pills on the right axis, floating swap/trade FAB. Market types: Categorical, Multi Categorical, Scalar, Multi Scalar (+ Futarchy/conditional). Lifecycle (docs): create, verify, mint/merge/redeem, buy/sell, report, dispute, resolve, provide liquidity; Reality.eth oracle, Kleros disputes, conditional tokens (ERC1155 wrapped to ERC20). EF kit boards (kit/dark-mode-board.webp etc.) show the target card patterns: Yes/No micro-buttons, three-way segmented, Up/Down big buttons with LIVE dot, head-to-head, single Yes/No, trade ticket (Amount, +$1/+$5/+$20/+$100, Confirm). Digest: products/explorer/docs.md.
Brief: "the general explorer for all Seer markets: find and trade across market types without specialising in any one: Seer's equivalent of Etherscan."
Thesis proposal: "Every market, one index: find it, read it, trade it in seconds." World: the **Index**: dense, precise, Etherscan-like: grid/table toggle, facet sidebar or filter bar, sortable columns (volume, liquidity, closing, status, age), market inspector drawer with step chart + kit trade ticket, bookmarks, recent/trending, address-like short ids. Motion minimal and crisp: live-ticking numbers, sparkline draw-in, row hover preview; NO heavy particles (this is the calm workhorse). Highest density of all products.
Must show all five EF kit card types, market type filters, categories row with arrows, status (Open/Resolving/Resolved), search + palette, Create Market CTA, detail with chart/outcomes/ticket (Connect Wallet gate), activity, liquidity.
Palette: neutrals + violet; outcome colors from the categorical set; status Open green, Resolving amber, Resolved grey. Tests: ui-quality-rules s12 + card type coverage, facet counts, table sort stability, 500-item virtualization performance.

# Seer's own vision for the Explorer (source: https://x.com/seer_pm/status/2111351009801089244, "Redesigning Seer for Information Finance (Part II of the Rebrand Contest)", 2026-10-17; text read by Claude in the browser)
- Seer moves from the 'prediction markets' label to INFORMATION FINANCE: not 'a place to trade' but 'a way of information discovery'. Less than 2% of markets produce information (The Block 2026). Vitalik's info-finance article: start with something people need to know and design a market that draws out information about it.
- Design for BOTH the trader placing a position AND the USER ASKING THE QUESTION. 'Not just Polymarket reskins.' Fresh ideas only.
- 'Seer app is the general explorer for all Seer markets: a place to find and trade across market types, without specialising in any one of them; Seer's equivalent of Etherscan.'
- 'A visitor should be able to tell WHO IS ASKING the question, WHAT THE MARKET IS ACTUALLY PRICING, and HOW TO USE THAT SIGNAL.' If somebody can understand why a market exists and what they can learn from it without first becoming a trader, the rebrand expresses the protocol.
- Direction: the agentic economy; consider useful features from that frame (agents reading/forecasting markets, machine-readable signals).
- Electric Foundation is the recognisable starting identity but flexible; the team wants the LINE DESIGN direction reversed vs the original EF line art (their preferred direction was shown in an image we could not see: treat line motifs as optional and unconfirmed).

Demands: (1) hero/first screen must be COMPACT and dense: market tiles visible immediately; any highlight/trending is a slim module (<= 150px) or a side column; no wasted space (apply space theory: group, align, rhythm), but also not cramped: organised and well aligned. (2) Yes and No must have DIFFERENT colours everywhere (tile, buttons, graphs, ticket): deviate from EF only where needed and state the reason in an EF-departures table; use colour theory (complementary/analogous harmony within EF's palette: violet, lavender, teal #2FD4C0, green #3DDC97, amber #F5B84A, orange, red #FF5C72, blue #5B9DFF; never the same colour for both sides; colour-blind safe with a second channel (shape/position/label)). Use more of EF's palette, not only violet. (3) Every graph must MEAN something at a glance: direct labels ('Yes 62% , up 4 pts in 7d'), axis in plain words, annotation of the event that moved it; the tile graph must show the winning percentage as clearly as the live donut (ref8) or better. Scalar markets (gwei etc.) use a refined 3D ribbon/tube line (ref11) with the estimate and range labelled. (4) Tiles redesigned: organised, aligned, apt colours; hover shows extra information in an organised compact panel (no wasted space, does not cover Yes/No), coin stacks animate on hover; trade buttons must LOOK like trade buttons (e.g. 'Buy Yes 62c' / 'Buy No 38c' with distinct colours), not fancy blobs. (5) Filters: well arranged, smooth animated dropdowns (hover/click, 150-200ms, ease-out, outside click/Escape closes, never overlapping the bar), organised icons, no heavy chips. (6) Header: redesigned, polished, aligned, with beautiful animated dropdown menus (Policies, App listing all Seer products with their glyph and real links). (7) Market page: price history as the mountain/ridge graph (ref10) or pipe graph (ref11) with matching EF colours; the TRADE WINDOW is redesigned (not a Google-Form) and is STICKY/FIXED in its column: it must NOT scroll away with the page: only the chart and the content below scroll; the wallet/trade section stays in view. (8) Information-finance layer (who is asking / what is priced / how to use the signal) stays, expressed compactly.

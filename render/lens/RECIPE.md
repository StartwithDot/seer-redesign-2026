# Lens sculpture — Blender 5.2 recipe (target = assets/reference-lens.webp)
Write render/lens/scene.py (bpy). Run: /Applications/Blender.app/Contents/MacOS/Blender -b -P render/lens/scene.py -- still   (writes render/lens/out.png)
Also support arg `anim` (frames -> render/lens/frames/f_####.png, see ANIMATION). 
GEOMETRY (build exactly, in metres):
1. RING: flat thick annulus. Outer radius 2.0, inner radius 1.55, depth (thickness along its axis) 0.55. Make by a cylinder with a boolean-cut inner cylinder, or a profile revolved. Bevel all edges radius ~0.04 (segments 4) with smooth shading. Matte ceramic: Principled BSDF base #EDEDF0, roughness 0.55, tiny clearcoat 0.1. Hole is open (see-through).
2. LENS: biconvex glass disc inside the ring, same axis, centred. Radius 1.5, centre thickness 0.55; build as intersection of two spheres of radius ~2.6 offset +-1.15 along the axis (boolean intersect) or a UV sphere scaled on the axis (scale z 0.28) — pick whichever gives a smooth crisp edge. Material: Principled, transmission 1, roughness 0.02, IOR 1.45, base colour violet #7C5CFF, volume absorption tint violet (density low) so the middle looks deep violet and edges lighter; thin bright edge. Add 4 tiny invisible-ish brackets? NO — the lens simply floats, held by nothing (as in reference).
3. Whole assembly (ring+lens) tilted: rotate so the ring axis points toward camera-right-up; ring seen as a big ellipse leaning ~35° from camera facing (like reference: opening faces left-up-forward, the ring leans on its lower-right edge on the floor, lowest point touches the floor).
4. FLOOR + BACKGROUND: infinite white seamless studio, floor slightly glossy (roughness 0.25, base white) giving a soft blurred reflection of the ring and a violet caustic/colour spill beneath the lens; background pure soft white gradient to pale grey at corners.
LIGHTING: large soft area light key from upper-left (6x6m), big soft fill from right, a strong narrow white strip light BEHIND the ring facing the camera through the lens to create the bright white inner halo like the reference, and a violet-tinted small area light under/behind to spill violet onto the white inner ring wall. World: white, strength 0.6. Contact shadow where ring meets floor.
CAMERA: 85 mm, f/8, framed 1440x900 (render 2880x1800 or 2400x1500 for the still); sculpture on the RIGHT ~55% of frame, left 40% empty white for text; sculpture slightly cropped bottom-right shadow area fine.
RENDER: Cycles, GPU Metal (fallback CPU), 128 samples still, OpenImageDenoise, AgX or Standard view transform with Look 'None', exposure so whites are bright (not grey), caustics on for the lens (reflective+refractive caustics true, clamp 10), light paths transmission bounces 12.
ANIMATION (only when arg `anim`): 96 frames at 24 fps (4 s) seamless loop, Cycles 48 samples + denoise, 1440x900, PNG frames. Motion (all cyclic, sin-based, frame 0 == frame 96): the key/rim strip light slides slowly from the left side of the lens to the right and back (position -x..+x), lens rotates +-4 degrees about its vertical axis and +-2 degrees tilt, the whole thing barely drifts. This makes the refraction, highlights and violet caustic travel slowly across the lens like light shifting through glass. No other motion.
ACCEPTANCE: side-by-side with reference — white ring clearly thick with soft bevel and bright inner halo, lens glassy violet with visible refraction/edge highlights, violet reflection on the floor, soft realistic shadows, no clipping, nothing grey or flat.

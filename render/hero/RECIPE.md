# Hero prism — Blender 5.2 recipe (reference: assets/reference-prism.webp; DO NOT copy its text/cards/labels — object and light only)
Overwrite render/hero/scene.py (ignore any old content). Modes via `-- still|anim`.
OBJECT (metres): a vertical double ring: two thin chrome torus rings (major radius 2.2, tube 0.035) 0.14 apart, standing upright on a low chrome disc base (radius 1.6, height 0.09 with beveled edge + a thinner second ring-shaped step). Inside the ring floats an elongated faceted glass crystal: a double pyramid (octahedron-like, height 3.4, width 2.0, 8 crisp facets, a slight irregular twist) rotated ~20° so facets catch light. Glass: Principled transmission 1, roughness 0.02, IOR 1.52, tint very pale violet; sharp bright facet edges (small bevel 0.01). Use Cycles caustics off for speed but keep transmission bounces >= 12.
LIGHT (the heart): a thin white beam (emissive thin cylinder radius 0.012 + soft glow) enters the crystal from the LEFT at ~8° upward angle starting beyond the left frame edge. Inside the crystal it splits; on the RIGHT a fan of ~36 thin rays diverges in a cone of ~22° (a few slightly curved), each an emissive ribbon/tube (radius 0.006–0.02, random per ray), gradient emission: near-white lilac at the crystal fading through violet #6C47FF to fully transparent before the frame edge; some rays reflect softly on the floor. Slight additive-looking bloom via a compositor Glare (fog glow, low threshold, size 6, mix -0.6) so rays glow softly. NO text, labels, numbers or cards anywhere.
FLOOR/WORLD: infinite seamless glossy white studio floor (roughness 0.18), soft reflections of object/rays, faint violet tint reflection under the rays; world white (pure #FFFFFF background and far floor fall to exactly 1.0 white — important: the video will be blended with CSS multiply on a white page). Standard view transform, exposure so whites clip to 255 only in the far background, not on the ring/crystal.
CAMERA: 70 mm, 1440x900 frame, slightly above, 3/4 view; crystal+ring centred at x≈62% of frame width; rays leave the frame on the right edge; left 40% is clean white (text overlay area), with soft fading shadow/reflection tones. Subtle depth of field (f/8).
RENDER: Cycles GPU Metal (CPU fallback), still 128 samples + OpenImageDenoise; anim 40 samples + denoise at 1440x900 (keep <= 50 s/frame).
ANIMATION (`anim`): 72 frames @24 fps, SEAMLESS LOOP (frame 72 == frame 0). Light must feel continuously passing through: each ray's gradient/emission pattern TRAVELS outward from the crystal (animate a texture-coordinate offset or per-ray emission phase = frame/72*2π so pulses of brighter light run along every ray left-to-right and repeat), the incoming beam also carries travelling pulses, overall ray intensity breathes ±10%, fan angle sways ±1.2°, crystal rotates ±2.5° about its vertical axis. Everything cyclic with sin/modulo. No other motion.
ACCEPTANCE: looks like a premium product-launch render; rays crisp and elegant not blurry; white is clean; chrome and glass believable; zero text.

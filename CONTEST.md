# POIDH bounty #38: Seer rebrand contest Part II (what the entrants must deliver and how it is judged)
Source: https://poidh.xyz/mainnet/bounty/38/data and Seer's X article (see seer_xpost.md, images seer_ef_line_direction.png, seer_xpost_stats.png).
- Prize: 0.5 ETH + 4,000 USDC for a non-internal winner. Deadline 23:59 Oct 23 2026 AoE. The Seer team also enters; the winner is chosen by Seer (team vote, likely informed by an Opportunity Market among traders).
- Task: design ALL SIX Seer interfaces with the Electric Foundation (EF) brand kit as the STARTING POINT (near-black tonal surfaces, violet primary, Inter Tight, line motif). Departures are allowed if explained. Seer wants fresh ideas, NOT Polymarket reskins, NOT generic crypto dashboards.
- The six surfaces and their roles: (1) Seer homepage: introduces Seer as the protocol beneath the apps and shows how they fit in one ecosystem. (2) Seer app: the general explorer of all markets, Seer's Etherscan. (3) Deep: many-outcome questions incl. markets for AI agents (agents forecast how human evaluators will judge a field, e.g. grant proposals). (4) Futarchy: conditional markets, forecast of a success measure if a proposal passes vs fails; informs a DAO, does not decide for it. (5) Opportunity Markets: traders forecast which submissions get selected; reviewers keep the final say. (6) Risk Pricing: market estimates of risk across protocols, compare side by side instead of one yes/no price.
- Strategic frame from the article: Information Finance (not "prediction markets"); design for the USER ASKING THE QUESTION as much as the trader; a visitor must tell who is asking, what the market prices, how to use the signal; understand why a market exists without becoming a trader; the agentic economy is the direction; the EF line motif direction must be reversed (one line left fanning out to many lines right).
- Claim must contain: preview image, X handle, short design rationale, explanation of EF departures, six labelled links to PUBLIC hosted demos. Judges open the six links and compare on first impression, design quality, comprehension, fit to the brief, EF use + justified departures, and whether it works.
- Competing entries seen: Moneygoblin (surge.sh, basic), Ones Laboratory (basic), both self-described drafts. Expect strong designers and the Seer team too.
